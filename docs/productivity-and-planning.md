# Productivity and Planning Skills

Workflows for turning goals, ideas, and operational information into structured plans and decisions.

## Included capabilities

- Product requirements, project sizing, roadmaps, and sprint planning
- OKR planning, Gantt planning, process documentation, and retrospectives
- Campaign planning, pricing strategy, customer retention, and SaaS analysis
- Meeting recaps, work reports, workload estimation, and structured minutes
- Flashcards, quizzes, interview practice, resumes, and personal planning
